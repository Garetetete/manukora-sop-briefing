# Monthly S&OP Briefing — March 2026

## Headline
Approve immediate inventory orders for four products to protect $97,665 of monthly revenue. Six products are draining stock rapidly and will run out starting in May 2026. 

## What sold well and what did not
Propolis Tincture 30ml is our strongest performer, with demand growing +40.0% across the measured window. This matters because we are phasing this product out, risking the loss of our fastest-growing item. 

Manuka Honey MGO 100+ 250g is our weakest performer, growing only +11.7% across the measured window. This matters because it is tying up capital; we have 2272 units above a healthy level, leaving $56,777 at retail trapped in excess inventory with 7.38 months of cover against a target of 2.

## What is at risk
Six products will run out of stock within three months, exposing a significant loss of sales. In May 2026, we will stock out of Manuka Honey MGO 514+ 500g exposing $34,316 per month, Manuka Honey MGO 850+ 500g exposing $29,477 per month, Bioactive Blend Energy 250g exposing $17,396 per month, Bioactive Blend Recovery 250g exposing $16,476 per month, and Propolis Tincture 30ml exposing $6,578 per month. In June 2026, Manuka Honey MGO 263+ 500g will run out, exposing $40,308 per month.

## Recommended actions
Order 650 units of Manuka Honey MGO 514+ 500g to be placed by April 2026. No order is currently placed, and with demand growing 9.3% per month, our projected cover is only 1.75 months against a target of 2. Replenishing now prevents a stockout in May 2026 and secures $34,316 of monthly revenue at stake.

Order 450 units of Manuka Honey MGO 850+ 500g to be placed by April 2026. Demand is growing 9.9% per month, dropping our projected cover to 1.58 months against a target of 2. Taking this action prevents a stockout in May 2026 and protects $29,477 of monthly revenue at stake.

Order 750 units of Bioactive Blend Energy 250g to be placed by April 2026. Because demand is growing 12.2% per month, our projected cover is 1.62 months against a target of 2. Approving this order avoids a May 2026 stockout and saves $17,396 of monthly revenue at stake.

Order 800 units of Bioactive Blend Recovery 250g to be placed by April 2026. Demand is growing 13.2% per month, leaving us with a projected cover of 1.42 months against a target of 2. Ordering now prevents a May 2026 stockout and protects $16,476 of monthly revenue at stake.

## Judgement calls
Investigate the channel performance for Manuka Honey MGO 100+ 250g. While pooled demand still grew, Shopify grew by 24 units while Amazon fell by 16 in the latest month. We need to identify and fix the underlying channel problem on Amazon.

Review the plan to discontinue Propolis Tincture 30ml after June 2026. Demand is growing 40% across the series, making it the fastest in the range. We must confirm if the phase-out decision was made with this trend visible, as we are cutting off our highest-growth item.

## Data quality
Confirm whether the launch dates or the extracts are wrong before we use this data for a real reorder decision. The brief states Bioactive Blend Immunity 250g, Bioactive Blend Energy 250g, and Bioactive Blend Recovery 250g all launched mid-January 2026 (mid-M2). However, the extract records 384 units, 272 units, and 248 units sold respectively in M1 (December 2025), before the stated launch. The assumption taken is that the trend is measured from M2 onwards as instructed, and the M1 figures are excluded from growth but left in the totals unchanged.

## Method

Demand is Shopify and Amazon pooled, because stock is shared. The most recent month is the sell-through baseline, and demand is projected forward at each SKU's own average growth rate for at most 3 months; beyond that it is held flat rather than compounded. Cover is simulated month by month, so an incoming order counts only in the month it arrives. Figures for the four months to date are observed; anything beyond them is modelled.
