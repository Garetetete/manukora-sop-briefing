# Monthly S&OP Briefing — March 2026

## Headline
Approve the recommended reorders by April 2026 to protect $97,665 of monthly revenue. Growth in larger sizes and functional blends is causing stockouts as early as May 2026, requiring immediate action even while our core honey ranges sit on excess inventory.

## What sold well and what did not
Manuka Honey MGO 263+ 250g was the strongest performer, generating $56,124 at retail from 1604 units sold this month. This highlights our inventory imbalance, as we currently hold 884 units above a healthy level, unnecessarily tying up $30,931 at retail. 

Propolis Tincture 30ml was the weakest product, selling 168 units for $5,878 at retail. This matters because demand grew 40.0% across its measured window, forcing us to decide whether to proceed with its planned discontinuation given the sudden surge in interest.

## What is at risk
Six products will stock out if we fail to act. By May 2026, we will run out of Manuka Honey MGO 514+ 500g exposing $34,316 per month, Manuka Honey MGO 850+ 500g exposing $29,477 per month, Bioactive Blend Energy 250g exposing $17,396 per month, Bioactive Blend Recovery 250g exposing $16,476 per month, and Propolis Tincture 30ml exposing $6,578 per month. By June 2026, we will run out of Manuka Honey MGO 263+ 500g, exposing $40,308 per month.

Order 650 units of Manuka Honey MGO 514+ 500g by April 2026. We must act now to cover a projected stockout in May 2026, as demand is growing 9.3% per month and a missed order puts $34,316 of monthly revenue at stake.

Order 800 units of Bioactive Blend Recovery 250g by April 2026. We need this stock to prevent a May 2026 stockout as demand is growing 13.2% per month, protecting $16,476 of monthly revenue at stake.

Review the decision to discontinue Propolis Tincture 30ml after June 2026. Demand is growing 40.0% across its measured window, representing the largest total change in the range, and we must confirm the phase-out was decided with this trend visible.

## Data quality
Verify the launch date or correct the data extract for the Bioactive Blend Immunity 250g, Bioactive Blend Energy 250g, and Bioactive Blend Recovery 250g before utilizing this data for future decisions. The brief states these products launched mid-January 2026, but the extract shows sales in December 2025 of 384 units, 272 units, and 248 units respectively. We assumed the trend should be measured from January 2026 onwards, excluding the December 2025 figures from growth calculations but leaving them in the totals unchanged.

## Method

Demand is Shopify and Amazon pooled, because stock is shared. The most recent month is the sell-through baseline, and demand is projected forward at each SKU's own average growth rate for at most 3 months; beyond that it is held flat rather than compounded. Cover is simulated month by month, so an incoming order counts only in the month it arrives. Figures for the four months to date are observed; anything beyond them is modelled.
